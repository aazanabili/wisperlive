# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Windows users who dictate text into any focused application. They need a low-friction way to start and stop speech capture without leaving their current task.

## Product Purpose

WhisperLive records speech through a global shortcut, turns it into polished text with Gemini, and pastes the result into the active application. Success means the utility stays out of the user's way while making its recording and processing state unmistakable.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, dependable. The product should feel like a premium desktop utility rather than a crowded settings form.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dashboard styling, noisy gradients, decorative animations, and ambiguous recording states. Do not make background operation feel hidden or unsafe.

## Design Principles

- Keep the primary dictation workflow visible without stealing focus.
- Use a clear visual hierarchy so configuration is easy to scan and safe to change.
- Make recording, processing, success, and error states unmistakable at a glance.
- Favor familiar Windows behaviors for tray operation, startup, and window management.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use strong color contrast, native keyboard-accessible controls, text labels alongside status color, and restrained state motion suitable for standard desktop accessibility needs.
